Our Story

Built for Players.
By Players.

8 Meter Mafia started with a simple idea: girls lacrosse deserved its own lifestyle brand — something built with the same passion that drives players to step into that arc and take their shot.

How It Started

From the Field to the Brand

8 Meter Mafia was born out of frustration. As lacrosse players ourselves, we were tired of seeing generic sports apparel that didn't speak to who we were — fierce competitors, teammates for life, and girls who carried the game everywhere we went.

The 8-meter arc is one of the most iconic spaces in women's lacrosse. It's where free position shots happen — where skill, nerve, and training meet under pressure. We named our brand after it because that's exactly what we're about: showing up when it counts.

We launched with a small collection of tees and hoodies, and the response from the lacrosse community was overwhelming. Within months, 8 Meter Mafia was being worn on sidelines, in hallways, and on campuses across the country.

Our Mission

More Than a Brand.
A Movement.

We exist to celebrate the girls and women who make girls lacrosse the fastest-growing sport in America. Whether you're a youth player just picking up a stick, a high school standout pushing for a D1 offer, or a college player competing at the highest level — you're Mafia.

Our mission is to give this community a brand that reflects who they are: strong, skilled, stylish, and unapologetically passionate about the game.

Every piece we design starts with a question: would a lacrosse girl actually love wearing this? If the answer's yes, it makes the cut. If not, back to the drawing board.

What We Stand For

Our Values

Compete

We celebrate the competitor in every player. The grind, the early morning practices, the extra shots after everyone else has gone home — that's what 8M is built for.

Sisterhood

Girls lacrosse creates lifelong bonds. We build our brand around that community — because the relationships you make on the field are the ones that last forever.

🔥
Grind

There are no shortcuts. We respect the work ethic it takes to compete at any level. Our gear is built for players who put in the hours, every single day.

Own Your Style

You don't have to choose between athletic and stylish. Our designs are made to transition from the field to anywhere your day takes you — without skipping a beat.

Quality First
